# Dr. Mehran apps

Two patient-facing health apps for Dr. Mehran.

| App | Folder | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KidneyWell**: kidney health tracking and education | [`apps/kidneywell`](apps/kidneywell) | Phase 1 foundation built |
| **Hypertension Hub**: blood pressure tracking and management | `apps/hypertension-hub` | Not started |

Both are Expo (React Native + TypeScript) apps for iOS and Android. See [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md) for the build plan and the decisions still open.
